FM5 Jump to Manufacturer expansion

With Azimuth closed, replace winmm.dll beside azimuth.exe with the included DLL.
Keep your existing INI and DAT files. No configuration changes are needed when
Frontend.JumpToManufacturer=1 (the default).

All car selectors: View opens Jump to Manufacturer. Includes Buy Cars, My Cars,
Designs/Paint, Career, Free Play, Rivals, lobby selectors and Marketplace, plus
the initial-purchase and ForzaVista demo variants.
Rim style lists: Y opens Jump to Manufacturer; View retains Setup manager.
Includes the original rim styles, All aftermarket rims and All stock rims.

All grids are alphabetical and open on the selected item's manufacturer.
My Cars skips the pinned current car when a regular brand entry is available.
Stock rims use car manufacturer names; aftermarket rims resolve native localized
part manufacturer names. Their logos come from the current database list.
Aftermarket tiles are white with dark text; stock-brand tiles remain dark.

Validation passed against a private mapped FM5 executable, including native
popup construction, localized text conversion, current-car pin handling,
rim row selection, repeated footer rebuilding, cancellation and patch rollback.
The expanded fixture covers all twelve native car-selector types, their own
lifecycle callbacks, filtered collections, prompt visibility, recommendations,
cancel/destruction cleanup and both car-selector XAML scenes.
Photo Mode footer, damage reset and altitude regression checks also passed.
Not yet tested in a running game.

Azimuth only; includes the existing FH2 and FM5 development profiles.
